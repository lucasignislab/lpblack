# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, chosen for fast delivery on Netlify and minimal runtime overhead.

## Users

Brazilian advertisers, affiliates, traffic managers and infoproduct businesses evaluating Ratoeira during the Black Friday campaign.

## Product Purpose

Capture qualified interest in the Black Friday offer for Ratoeira Ads, Pages or Hub through a short, direct landing page and a Netlify-compatible lead form.

## Positioning

Ratoeira connects media decisions to real tracked data and combines tracking and fast pages in one performance-focused ecosystem.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The page must remain fast and responsive from small phones through large desktop monitors.
- The primary flow is a short lead form collecting name, professional email, WhatsApp and product interest.
- The campaign price, dates and final video are not confirmed and must not be invented.
- Form submission must work when deployed to Netlify.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Ratoeira logo and the supplied design system. Copy is direct, confident and performance-oriented. The existing landing-page screenshot is the visual authority for this recreation.

## Product Principles

- Make the offer and next action understandable within the first viewport.
- Collect only the information needed to qualify interest.
- Prefer measured, verifiable language over absolute performance claims.
- Keep the experience functional when tracking scripts are unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus, labeled inputs, sufficient contrast and layouts that do not require horizontal zoom.
